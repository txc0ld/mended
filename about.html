Advice is the easy part

A recommendation can sound right and still fail when the roster changes, the floor is busy or cash is tight. Owners do not need another document that assumes unlimited time and attention.

Operator-to-operator means practical

The work starts with how the business really runs. Decisions account for members, staff, delivery and the numbers together. What gets built must be clear enough for the team to use and strong enough to keep working when the owner steps back.

What Mended believes.

Good consulting should leave the business clearer, steadier and less dependent on outside help.

01

Start with the constraint

The loudest problem is not always the one holding the business back. Find the cause before adding more activity.

02

Make it usable

A system should help the team make the next decision. If it only looks good in a document, it is not finished.

03

See the whole business

Team, operations, sales and cash pressure affect each other. Fixing one in isolation can move the problem elsewhere.

04

Hand ownership back

The end point is a business the owner and team can run. The consultant should not become another permanent dependency.
