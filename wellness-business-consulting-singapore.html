Questions from Singapore operators.

How do you assess whether a Singapore lease is viable?

Mended tests the rent against realistic capacity, pricing, staffing and retention, then models the pressure created by the lease term and renewal point. Legal review and property advice still sit with qualified local advisers.

Can you help with foreign instructor staffing constraints?

Yes. The operating work considers pass requirements, local hiring, instructor utilisation, employment cost and service continuity. Immigration and employment advice must come from qualified Singapore advisers.

How can a studio grow without discounting?

The focus is a premium offer that is easy to explain and consistently delivered. Mended also examines corporate wellness and insurer channels where they fit, while protecting direct member pricing and retention.
