Hiring and team

Good people should make the business stronger, not create another layer for the owner to carry.

What you are dealing with

Every roster gap lands with you. New starters learn by watching whoever is on shift. Strong team members leave because expectations, feedback and progression are unclear.

What Mended does

Clarifies the roles the business actually needs, tightens hiring and onboarding, and puts useful management habits into the week. Responsibilities become visible and follow-up stops relying on memory.

What changes

People know what good work looks like and who owns each decision. The team can handle more without escalating every detail, giving the owner room to lead the business.

Operations and systems

A process only counts when the team can use it on a busy day without calling the owner.

The drag

Instructions live in messages, documents and one person's head. Problems are handled differently each time. The week fills with checking, chasing and preventable rework.

The work

Mended maps the recurring work, removes unnecessary steps and sets clear ownership. The right checklists, tools, meetings and measures are built around how the venue actually runs.

The result

The operation becomes easier to see and manage. Staff can act without guessing, issues surface earlier, and the business keeps moving when the owner is not in the room.

Sales and revenue

More leads do not repair weak pricing, unclear offers or members leaving through the back door.

01

What you are seeing

Enquiries stall, discounts become the default and the membership mix no longer supports the cost of delivery. Staff avoid sales conversations or handle them differently. Existing members drift away without a clear response.

02

What Mended does

Reviews the offer, pricing, packages, sales path and retention habits together. The work focuses on what members value, what the team can sell honestly and what the numbers need to support.

03

What changes

The offer is easier to understand and the team has a clear way to sell it. Pricing decisions have a reason behind them. Retention becomes an operating habit, not a rescue campaign after cancellations rise.

Reset and crisis turnaround

When cash, members or energy are running out, the order of the work matters. Stabilise first. Rebuild second.

When this is the right work

The business is losing money, membership is sliding, bills are stacking up or the owner cannot see a workable next move. There may be several visible problems, but limited time and cash to address them.

How Mended responds

Gets a clear view of cash, commitments, members, staffing and delivery. Immediate leaks are contained, decisions are ranked by urgency, and a practical recovery plan is put into action with the owner.

What changes

The owner knows what must happen now, what can wait and what needs to stop. Once the business is stable, the underlying team, operating or revenue problems can be rebuilt without panic driving every decision.
