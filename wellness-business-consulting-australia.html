Questions from Australian operators.

Can you help a studio stop competing on price?

Yes. The work looks at positioning, pricing, membership structure, sales conversations and retention together. The aim is to give the right members a clear reason to choose and stay with the studio without relying on constant discounts.

How do you help an owner get off the timetable?

Mended maps the work the owner is carrying, separates delivery from management, and builds clearer roles, onboarding, rosters and operating routines. The change is staged so service quality does not depend on one sudden hire.

Should we open a second location?

Only when the first site has the margin, leadership and repeatable systems to support it. Mended tests the case for expansion, including demand, staffing, owner capacity and the cash pressure created before the second site settles.
