Questions from Bali operators.

How do you work with an Australian owner managing Bali at a distance?

Mended establishes a clear operating rhythm between the owner and the local team, including decision rights, reporting, cash controls and regular management conversations. The aim is to make distance manageable without turning every issue into a message to Australia.

Can you help with local partnership and licensing structures?

Mended can help the owner understand how the structure affects control, responsibilities, cash and growth plans. Local legal, tax and licensing advice must come from qualified Indonesian advisers, and Mended works alongside them rather than replacing them.

Can an existing Bali team be trained to support another site?

Yes, when the operating model is made teachable first. Mended helps document the work, train managers, set service standards and test whether the current team can reproduce the experience before another site or franchise is committed.
