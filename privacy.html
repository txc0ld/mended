What the enquiry form collects

When you send an enquiry, Mended collects the information you enter: your name, email, optional phone number, business name, optional website or Instagram address, business type, location, number of locations, team size, optional monthly revenue range, what needs fixing, preferred start time, your message and consent confirmation.

Do not include health information, payment details or other sensitive personal information in the message field.

Why it is collected

Mended uses this information to read and respond to your enquiry, decide whether the work is a suitable fit, prepare for the first conversation, scope a possible engagement, keep a record of the conversation and meet legal obligations.

Enquiry details are not sold. They are not added to marketing emails unless you separately agree to receive them.

Where the information goes

The enquiry form is delivered through Formspree. A submission is stored in the Formspree inbox and sent to an authorised Mended email inbox. Access should be limited to people who need the information to respond or provide the service.

Formspree may also collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, access time and referring website to deliver and secure its service. Formspree operates in the United States and other countries, so form information may be processed outside Australia. Read the Formspree privacy policy for its current practices.

If Mended later connects the form to another customer management or email system, this policy must be updated before that system is used.

How long it is kept

Enquiry information is kept for up to 24 months after the last contact so Mended can follow the conversation and understand earlier scope. It is then deleted from the active inbox and Formspree account unless a longer period is required by law, needed for a current contract, or necessary to resolve a dispute.

If an enquiry becomes a client engagement, business and transaction records may be kept for the period required by Australian law.

Access, correction and removal

You can ask what personal information Mended holds about you, ask for a correction, withdraw consent, or ask for your enquiry information to be deleted. Some records may need to be retained where the law requires it.

Send a request with the subject line Privacy request. Include the email address used on the enquiry so the record can be found.
