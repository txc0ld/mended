What most consultants sell

Most consultants in this space sell one of two things: marketing and leads, or gym-specific operating playbooks. Either can help. Neither sees the full operation.

What Mended does

Mended works on the whole business, and has run one. Team, systems, sales and cash pressure are treated as connected problems, not separate projects.

Straight answers before the first call.

What does a wellness business consultant do?

A wellness business consultant looks at how the whole operation runs, finds what is holding it back, and helps fix the team, systems, sales or financial pressure causing the problem.

How is this different from a gym marketing agency?

A gym marketing agency mainly sells lead generation and member acquisition. Mended can work on sales, but also fixes hiring, delivery, pricing, retention and the operating systems behind them.

What size business does Mended work with?

Mended works with owner-operated boutique businesses, from a single gym or studio to multi-site groups, retreats and franchise operators. The fit depends more on the problem and the owner's readiness to act than location count.

Do you work with businesses outside Australia?

Yes. Mended works with operators in Bali and Singapore as well as Australia. If your business is elsewhere, the first call will confirm whether the work and local conditions are a good fit.

What does an engagement look like and how long does it run?

Work starts by diagnosing the real constraint, then rebuilding what is weak and handing over a system your team can run. Timing depends on the scope, urgency and number of areas involved.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is scoped after the first call. Cost depends on the problem, the amount of hands-on work required and how much of the business is involved. You will receive a clear scope before committing.
